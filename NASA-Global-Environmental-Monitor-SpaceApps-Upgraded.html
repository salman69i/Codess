DATA & METHODS CENTER

Evidence, definitions, provenance & limitations

This application distinguishes verified external observations from demonstration values. NASA POWER temperature is fetched directly from NASA's public API when the selected location is available. NASA GIBS/Worldview imagery is used for Earth-observation visualization. Other metrics remain explicitly labelled demonstration/connector data until a verified point-sampling service is connected.

Scientific definitions

Uncertainty & limitations

  • Observed data and model projections are different products and are labelled separately.
  • Uncertainty bands in this prototype are visual analytical ranges; they are not official NASA confidence intervals unless the underlying dataset supplies them.
  • Correlation is descriptive and does not establish causation.
  • Sea level must be specified as anomaly, absolute height, or rate with a reference frame; this dashboard uses a demo rate until a verified altimetry point service is connected.
  • CO₂ should identify XCO₂ or another exact product variable; ppm alone is not sufficient provenance.
  • Ozone values should identify total-column product, retrieval algorithm, quality flags and units.
  • Soil-moisture anomaly requires a documented climatological baseline, depth and quality filtering.
  • CO and N₂O require documented atmospheric level/column definitions and averaging periods.
NASA SPACE APPS PROJECT

MISSION MODE

A five-step story for judges and users: observe → compare → analyze → listen → verify.

01

SELECT A LOCATION

Click the real map, search a city/coordinate, or use browser geolocation. The selected latitude and longitude become the analysis target.

02

OBSERVE EARTH SYSTEM SIGNALS

Inspect temperature, atmospheric CO₂, sea level, ozone, soil moisture anomaly, carbon monoxide and nitrous oxide with their units and definitions.

03

TRAVEL THROUGH TIME

Move the Earth Time Machine through the selected period. Historical observations, current values and projections are visually separated.

04

COMPARE & SONIFY

Compare locations and use sonification as an analytical cue: pitch, rhythm and modulation encode normalized value/trend. The chart remains the authoritative numeric view.

05

VERIFY THE EVIDENCE

Open Data & Methods to see source, mission/instrument target, metric definition, update status and limitations. Never confuse demonstration values with measured NASA observations.
